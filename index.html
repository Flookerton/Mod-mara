How it works

Every picture is a grid that has been bent (the warp) with a shape drawn in each tile (the motif), or one of the classic illusions. Everything is black ink on paper, like a print.

  1. Press Surprise me for a fresh random picture.
  2. Click the small pictures to change the warp, motif or illusion. Drag the sliders to fine tune.
  3. The dice next to a section shuffles just that part. The lock keeps a part fixed when you press Surprise me.
  4. Press play to watch a pattern move. Each one loops without a seam. Record loop saves it as a video.
  5. Press the heart to keep a picture in your favorites. They live in this browser only, so use Back up all to save them to a file.
  6. Save the picture as a PNG, or save its settings file.

Settings files and seeds

A settings file (.json) describes a picture completely, so loading it brings back exactly the same picture. The number under the picture only seeds the random choices in this app. On its own it will not recreate a picture made with another tool.
